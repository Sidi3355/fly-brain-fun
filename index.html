    About the data

    Everything here is real

    Every neuron position, cell type, brain region, connection, neuron shape and microscope image on this site comes from public FlyWire and FAFB data. Neuronaut is an independent visualisation. It is not affiliated with the FlyWire Consortium.

    Connectome
    FlyWire v783, the proofread whole-brain connectome: 139,255 neurons, 54.5M synapses. Dorkenwald et al., Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain, Nature 2024.
    Cell types
    Schlegel et al., Whole-brain annotation and multi-connectome cell typing, Nature 2024 (flywire_annotations).
    Microscopy
    FAFB volume: Zheng et al., A complete electron microscopy volume of the brain of adult Drosophila melanogaster, Cell 2018. 7,062 sections, 21 million images.
    Synapses & transmitters
    Buhmann et al., Nature Methods 2021; Eckstein et al., Cell 2024. Transmitters are predicted from EM, not measured.
    Brain regions
    Neuropil meshes from FlyWire, nomenclature after Ito et al., Neuron 2014.
    Simulation
    Leaky integrate-and-fire model after Shiu et al., A Drosophila computational brain model reveals sensorimotor processing, Nature 2024, re-run here on v783.
