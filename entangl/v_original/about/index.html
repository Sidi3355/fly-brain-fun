Entangl/About Entangl
ENTANGL

The site keeps changing. Its knowledge should keep up.

A data center is designed by one group, built by another, and run by people who inherit the decisions. Each handover can leave them searching for the reason behind the work.

We are building Entangl to keep that knowledge connected. To the equipment. To the procedure. To the people who need to make the next decision.

Procedures are our core today because that is where site information has to become usable work. Our direction is the full lifecycle: design, handover, maintenance, operations, customer oversight, and the services that keep the facility running.
