Signals and integration

Identify the BMS, EPMS, or SCADA source, required points, units, timestamps, update rate, and supported connection path.

02

Data quality

Test stale, missing, mislabelled, and conflicting readings. A suggested explanation should change when the evidence changes.

03

Triage and response

Agree grouping, priority, ownership, acknowledgement, procedure references, and the handoff to remote or field teams.

04

Control authority

Scope observation, analysis, recommendations, and any equipment-control function separately. Define the human authorization and system responsibilities explicitly.

BUYER QUESTIONS

The questions
behind the decision.

Get the details clear before committing to a rollout.

Security and deploymentPricing and scope
Does this replace our BMS or EPMS?

The starting point can be an analysis and workflow layer alongside those systems. Confirm which system produces the signals, where operators acknowledge events, and how Entangl fits the screens and processes already in use.

Which integrations are supported?

Bring the system, available interface, and point list. We will confirm the current connection path and configuration required for your environment. The name of a protocol alone does not establish that the complete workflow is ready.

Can it identify the root cause?

It can support an investigation by relating signals and site context within the agreed scope. Test suggested explanations against known incidents and inspect the evidence. Missing or conflicting data should remain visible rather than becoming certainty.

Does it automatically control equipment?

Observation, analysis, suggested responses, and equipment control are separate scopes. Do not infer control authority from a telemetry demonstration. Any control function needs an explicitly agreed design and authorization boundary.

Can an issue include an EOP and an iPad handoff?

That is part of the connected response direction. Demonstrate the procedure version, operator review, assignment, field handoff, and returned evidence for the proposed deployment.

What about a countdown to failure?

A response deadline and a physical prediction answer different questions. A physical forecast needs validated inputs, assumptions, and testing for that equipment and operating condition. This example does not predict time to failure.

WORKFLOW SCOPE

Telemetry is scoped with your deployment. Confirm signal access, point mapping, timestamps, latency, and the observation-versus-control boundary.
