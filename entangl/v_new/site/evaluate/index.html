Plan my evaluation
01

Pick work worth improving.

Choose representative procedures and reviews, including complex work where your engineers spend meaningful time. Include known issues and valid examples.

02

Measure the whole job.

Track drafting, review, correction, and handoff effort. Record the issues found and missed, false positives, and whether people can complete the workflow.

03

Count the cost of getting there.

Include customer time, implementation, integrations, support, and the subscription in the decision.

04

Agree the next step before you start.

Define what earns a wider rollout, what needs more work, and when to stop. Record the owners of the operational, security, and budget decisions.

05

Take the plan to your team.

Use the evaluation brief to align operations, IT, security, procurement, and the budget holder around the same scope.
