Establish the program

Start from the assets, applicable manuals, site policy, and current maintenance history. Retain the reason for a proposed interval.

02

Coordinate the window

Check affected systems, customer restrictions, related work, and resource availability. A calendar gap alone does not establish a safe window.

03

Prepare the package

Identify the procedure, approvals, qualified people, vendors, parts, and evidence required to complete the work.

Use accepted findings and completed work history to review future tasks and intervals under the site’s change process.

BUYER QUESTIONS

The questions
behind the decision.

Get the details clear before committing to a rollout.

Security and deploymentPricing and scope
Is maintenance planning available today?

It is a planned workflow. This page is an interactive concept for discussing the proposed direction. Ask for the current availability, development scope, responsibilities, and commitments before including it in a rollout plan.

Where would task frequencies come from?

The proposal connects a task and interval to the applicable equipment guidance and site policy. Engineers must verify the equipment applicability, operating conditions, requirements, and any proposed deviation.

Can this work with our existing CMMS?

Define which system owns scheduling, work orders, completion, and the authoritative maintenance record. The discussion should identify the export or integration work needed, rather than assuming a connector is already available.

Can it identify conflicting work?

The proposed direction uses equipment relationships, related work, and customer restrictions to help expose conflicts. The example covers one simple relationship. Real coverage depends on validated dependencies and the rules included in scope.

Can we build the first-year plan before a new site goes live?

That is a useful planning use case. Begin with the available equipment and documents, then review the program as design information becomes the installed site and commissioning results become available.

What about vendors and spare parts?

Bring personnel qualifications, vendor availability, required parts, and other prerequisites into the work-package design. Confirm which records are managed in Entangl and which remain in specialist systems.
