Entangl/Implementation and support
ENTERPRISE

Start with one site. Make the work useful.

Build the rollout around a workflow your team can evaluate. Agree the data, the people, and the result before expanding.

Plan your first site
01

Choose the first job.

Pick a representative procedure or review task, an operational owner, and the outcome the evaluation needs to prove.

02

Prepare and check the site information.

Collect the agreed documents. Review equipment and connections. Give missing information a named owner.

03

Fit the process.

Check templates, approval roles, exports or integrations, and the final record your team needs.

04

Train on actual work.

Use the tasks people will perform, with a clear first action and a route for help.

05

Review the result.

Measure the work completed and the effort it took. Resolve the important gaps and agree the next site or workflow.

How much time will our team need?

The plan should show document preparation, site validation, IT work, testing, and training. We will estimate those tasks for the agreed scope.

How long does setup take?

It depends on data readiness, site scope, integrations, and the people available. We will agree a schedule once those are clear.
